# Lab report template

Copy this template into your own evidence directory when running a lab. Do not pre-fill actual results from the notes' expected observations.

- Date and owner:

- Predicted observation:
- Actual observation, timestamps, logs/queries:
- Invariant result:
- Recovery action:
- Reconciliation result:
- Resource cleanup:
- What the experiment proves:
- What it does not prove:
- Remaining uncertainty and next test:

## Decision
Record whether to keep, revise, or reject the hypothesis. A failed experiment can be useful evidence; do not rewrite the prediction after seeing the result.

## Learning assessment
Explain the mechanism without notes, draw the boundary, compare an alternative, and answer the chapter's fresh assessment. Record corrections and schedule recall from the actual study date.
